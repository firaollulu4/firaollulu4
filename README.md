## `whoami`

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, applying my hardware and signals background to cybersecurity. My ECE foundation gives me an edge in areas most software-only security people don't touch — RF and signal analysis, hardware-level system behavior, and communication protocols — which I'm channeling into building tools like HackRF-based SDR setups and researching satellite communication security. On the software side, I focus on reverse engineering and malware analysis, breaking down systems to understand exactly how they work. Beyond security, I create animated 3D ads and build automation workflows with n8n.
